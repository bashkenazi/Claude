# Weekly long/short idea screen

Each week: 3 new longs and 5 new shorts from the Tier 1-2 Stocks universe. These are names to research, not trades.

Ranking (percentile blend):
- 50% Signal score (store/signal_score: revenue surprise, non-plan insider selling, earnings yield, crowding, volatility)
- 25% fresh beat: latest revenue surprise minus the company's median of the prior 8 (post-earnings drift)
- 25% expected next-quarter beat (store/beat_model)
Longs = top of blend, shorts = bottom; skip names with fewer than 6 prints of history; at most one per subsector;
skip anything in ledger.json from the last 8 weeks.
